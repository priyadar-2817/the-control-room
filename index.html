The psychology underneath

How much should an agent do before it checks with you?

Agents don't just answer. They plan, spend, send and book. The design question is no longer what the AI says, but how far it should go on its own.

The Control Room sorts AI interaction patterns by the psychological need each serves: Orientation, Uncertainty, Control, Trust, Effort and Delegation. Each is placed on a spectrum of who is driving, from human leads to AI hands back.

In Play, you follow an agent through a real job across 8 moments. Each shows what the agent should do, the pattern and psychology behind it, and what breaks without it.

Patterns marked as gaps (clarifying questions, access requests, dry runs, budget guards, teams of agents and more) are areas existing collections rarely cover.

The scenario, venues, people and amounts are made up. Product names in the library only say where a pattern can be seen.

Designed and written by Priyadarshi, a product designer working where behavioural psychology meets product design.
